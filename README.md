# DOME

DOME is a data ecosystem for the age of AI. It gathers everything a life takes in and gives out, now scattered across devices, drives and clouds, and organizes it: every item fingerprinted, duplicates removed, original dates and sources kept. AI agents then work with it under strict rules, mapping how people, places, things and ideas connect.

**The problem:** our data is fragmented, and the value it creates is captured by others. DOME holds it in trust, so the people it reflects can see themselves clearly, understand their needs and their impact, and share in the value it creates.

DOME is the first part of a wider economic ecosystem aimed at enough for everyone. The archive engine works today; the agents and knowledge graph are being built.

---

## The bigger picture

**Nothing exists in isolation, and data is what makes the interdependence visible.** Every person, place, thing and idea is inherently plural, part of a larger whole.

DOME's scope is everything: every input to a life and every output, each output feeding back as the next input. Seen whole, that cycle reveals how much of our world the mind creates, and what is actually there.

**Its purpose is awareness.** When the parts of a whole see themselves clearly, they understand their needs, their value and their effect on others, for better or worse, and can govern themselves, democratically.

**Value comes from being, not only from producing.** Any data, however passive, helps the whole rebalance: matching resources to real needs, reducing waste and overconsumption, acting on climate. Growth still happens; what grows changes. The aim is a dividend for being, not only for working, and a path beyond compulsory work, overconsumption, greed and waste.

DOME continues more than a decade of work by [Jennifer Lyn Morone](https://jenniferlynmorone.com), from *Jennifer Lyn Morone™ Inc.*, the artwork in which she incorporated herself, to RadicalxChange.

## Principles

- **Held in trust.** Private material is encrypted and read only by agents under the rules of the lives it reflects. Public material is shared deliberately.
- **Provenance is kept.** True creation dates are preserved, and every item is labelled as original work, a conversion or AI-generated.
- **Relational, not individual.** The graph holds people, places, things and ideas as relationships, never as isolated records.
- **Consent before connection.** Others who appear in the data are recognised and asked, not assumed.
- **Allocation by need, not only by contribution.**
- **Proof over claims.** Every agent job has written acceptance checks; an agent's report of success is not evidence of success.
- **An open lens space.** Any discipline, tradition or way of knowing can be applied where it illuminates.

## Still to design

1. **Consent and identity:** how everyone who appears in the data is recognised, asked and given control.
2. **HAL's mechanics:** how contribution and need are understood fairly, and how allocation reaches each part.
3. **Collective governance:** how the parts of the whole decide together.
4. **Data-intake connectors:** the many sources a life's data comes from.
5. **Privacy-preserving shared compute:** creating shared value without raw data leaving where it is held.
6. **Legal form:** a data cooperative, data trust or perpetual purpose trust.
